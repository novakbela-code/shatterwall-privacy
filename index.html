ShatterWall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-18 · Applies to ShatterWall for Meta Quest, version 1.0 and later

ShatterWall is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest developed by GyorgyNovak ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. ShatterWall has no user accounts, no online or multiplayer features, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send any data from your headset to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your headset

To work properly, the game keeps a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • your game settings (for example audio volume and video options),
  • your best Overflow round,
  • whether you have finished the tutorial.

This data never leaves your headset and is not linked to your identity. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Meta platform

When the game starts, it asks the Meta Quest platform on your headset whether you own a valid license (an "entitlement check"). We only receive a yes/no answer and do not receive or store your Meta user ID, name or any other account information. The purchase, your Meta account and the headset itself are handled by Meta under its own terms and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Please follow Meta's age requirements for using Meta Quest devices.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the game. If you have a question or request regarding your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ShatterWall adds features that handle personal information (for example online multiplayer), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
